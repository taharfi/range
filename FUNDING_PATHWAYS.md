## Practical funding assessment

The most plausible near-term support is feedback and distribution from an ecosystem/tooling partner, followed by funding only if the pilot demonstrates recurring demand. Jupiter fit should be supported by actual quote/execution utility, and Meteora fit by better LP decisions or retention; both are presently hypotheses. The [2025 Meteora annual report](https://ir.meteora.ag/assets/Meteora_Annual_Token_Holder_Report_2025.pdf) emphasizes launch infrastructure and integrations in 2026, so SOL/USDC accounting alone should not be assumed a current funding priority.

Before any application, verify a current official intake, deadline, eligible scope, legal entity requirements, award versus investment terms, and budget. Funding milestones should concern opt-in users, repeat use, accounting coverage and observable user benefit—not more pools collected. Do not buy data or pivot into DBC just to match an expired sponsor prize.
