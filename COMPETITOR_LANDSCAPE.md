# Competitor landscape

Research date: **2026-10-20**. Scope: the four proposed LP products, not token launches or generic trading terminals. Public documentation verifies advertised availability and definitions, not execution reliability, customers, returns or revenue. We did not connect wallets or test funded execution. Price unknown means unverified, never free by assumption.

## What is already implemented

| Proposed capability | Public counterexamples | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Pool selection/fee screening | C01/C03/C04/C05 | Crowded; a generic decision dashboard is weak differentiation. |
| Wallet rankings/copy LP | C02/C04/C05 | Already offered; public-chain visibility does not imply demand for being copied. |
| Reusable strategies/rebalancing | C03/C05; C06/C08 adjacent | Strategy configuration is established. **Out-of-sample persistence validation** across comparable cohorts remains unverified, not confirmed absent. |
| Jupiter-powered zaps/claim conversion | C04; native C01 entry/exit tools | Jupiter branding alone provides no differentiation. |
| Hold benchmarks/risk metrics | C03/C07 | Explicitly documented. Not a defensible claimed missing feature. |
| Transaction-checked P&L | C05 closed copies; C07 documents cash-flow/gas accounting | Existing competition. Arbitrary-wallet coverage remains a documented constraint in C05. |
| Independent replay with explicit event/price coverage and execution costs | Our prior work exposes difficulty; competitors disclose parts | Potential verification gap, but no proof that all competitors lack it or users will pay. |

Native expansion is another threat: the official [DLMM Pro waitlist](https://www.meteora.ag/waitlist/dlmm-pro) is open for users/builders, and [October 17 team update](https://proposals.meteora.ag/t/update-referral-staking-expert-contributors/3394) discusses its announcement. Waitlist access is not proof of general production availability. Do not freeze product assumptions at the 2024 feature set.
