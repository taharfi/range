## Decision for this experiment

Use filtered current-account scans for the free bounded demonstration, then verify owner-event links with both the API and transaction decoding. This succeeded without buying infrastructure. It does not solve historical wallet discovery. The next research cohort should come from PositionCreate **and PositionClose** events over a predeclared slot interval, not from current open accounts or profitable-looking wallets.

Exact full-history costs cannot be estimated credibly until transaction density, retry rate, bytes, provider retention and decoder coverage are measured. Local disk/CPU/network costs are not metered here. No live trading, custody or transaction submission was used.
