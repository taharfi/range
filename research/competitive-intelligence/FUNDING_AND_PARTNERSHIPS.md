## Proposed build-versus-partner posture for review

Integrate documented protocol/quote infrastructure after existing authorized tests; avoid rebuilding signing, transaction indexing and broad market-data warehousing for a speculative feature set. Potential partners can help validate definitions and distribution, but independent methodology and caveats should remain inspectable. Any future partner must explicitly permit the relevant API use, caching and derived-data distribution. This sprint recommends no purchase and sends no outreach.
