# Competitor workflow and commercial review

Checked **2026-10-21**. This broad product review complements [REA_COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md](REA_COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md) and [COMPETITOR_ARCHITECTURES.md](COMPETITOR_ARCHITECTURES.md), which prioritize ten technical targets. Documentation describes vendor-supported workflows; it does not prove successful funded execution. Unknown is neither absent nor free. Numerical returns in marketing are excluded.

## Infrastructure vendors: different jobs, not interchangeable APIs

The 19 infrastructure rows in [COMPETITORS.csv](COMPETITORS.csv) map execution, archive/streams, market observations, query warehouses, explorers and signing. Detailed [ecosystem map](ECOSYSTEM_MAP.md) links every group to current primary documentation. A market-data provider's wallet/trader P&L does not imply DLMM inventory accounting. A transaction RPC does not supply historical USD marks automatically. A protocol's aggregate volume does not establish paying demand for an analytics product.

Fresh limitations with strategic consequences: [Pyth](https://docs.pyth.network/) now says Hermes requires a key following the 2026-08-26 upgrade; [Flipside's former docs](https://docs.flipsidecrypto.xyz/) redirect to an enterprise-AI site; SQD docs timed out. Do not budget these as anonymously accessible, equivalent data sources based on past research. No subscription was purchased and no competitor code was integrated.
