# Recommended Direction

## Primary direction: Focused LP Decision Intelligence

RANGE should become the evidence layer between a user's current Meteora position and the next action: HOLD, REBALANCE or EXIT. It should show current inventory/range state, amount-specific Jupiter conversion quotes, observable costs, unavailable costs, quote age, strategy assumptions and confidence.

## Why this direction

- Reuses working RANGE infrastructure.
- Addresses recurring decision and automation pain without assuming automation is the first product.
- Differentiates through explicit accounting boundaries and uncertainty rather than feature count.
- Creates a natural handoff to Meteora/Jupiter execution without custody.
- Can be tested in weeks with measurable behavior.

## Two alternatives

1. **Modular LP platform:** pursue after the decision brief proves repeat use; add alerts, saved positions and strategy modules.
2. **Neutral LP data/decision API:** pursue if partner interviews show wallet, alert or ecosystem tools will pay for normalized evidence.

## Explicit non-recommendations now

- Do not start with all-in-one execution.
- Do not claim verified historical profitability.
- Do not launch unrestricted copytrading.
- Do not build a full backtester until data coverage and counterfactual validation improve.
