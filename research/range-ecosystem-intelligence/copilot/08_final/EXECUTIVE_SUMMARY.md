# RANGE Ecosystem Intelligence: Executive Summary

**Research through:** 2026-10-21

## Coverage

- 30+ ecosystem catalog entries across LP tools, protocols and infrastructure.
- 12 competitor deep dives.
- 8 dated LP Army strategy cards with linked public sources.
- 43 first-hand/public pain records preserved from prior research; the 60-record target was not reached.
- Official Meteora developer, Data API, SDK, program, agent, MCP and Invent resources reviewed.
- Public Meridian repository inspected; REA was unavailable and not installed.

## Main findings

1. The all-in-one category is already crowded by overlapping discovery, analytics, automation and copy products.
2. LP Agent, Revert and RANGE's own audits show that accounting definitions and missing flows matter more than another headline PnL number.
3. LP Army strategy evidence is heterogeneous and often incomplete; self-reported returns are not validation.
4. Automation has repeated demand signals but adds custody, signer, slippage, failure recovery and support risk.
5. Jupiter and Meteora integrations are technically meaningful for quote-aware decision support, but current quotes do not solve historical accounting.
6. The strongest evidence-backed next direction is focused LP Decision Intelligence, designed to expand modularly only after repeated use is measured.

## Strategic recommendation

Keep RANGE read-only and decision-focused. Validate whether a neutral, cost-aware, uncertainty-explicit HOLD/REBALANCE/EXIT brief changes active LP decisions and earns repeat use. Do not build custody, copytrading or autonomous execution before that evidence exists.
