# Market Gaps

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-21

## Evidence-supported gaps

### 1. Decision support that exposes uncertainty and cost

Users ask for clearer inventory, range and metric interpretation. Native and third-party products cover basic charts and monitoring, but public evidence does not establish a neutral, all-in cost-aware decision brief. RANGE already has the read-only foundation and Jupiter quote boundary.

**Gap strength:** medium. Existing competitors overlap; differentiation is a hypothesis.

### 2. Comparable strategy descriptions

LP Army strategies mix bid-ask, curve, spot, ape-in, technical indicators and bear-market controls, but visible cards often omit complete assumptions and rules. A strategy comparison layer could normalize range, monitoring, cost and failure assumptions without claiming profitability.

**Gap strength:** medium-low. Requires more full article access and user testing.

### 3. Trustworthy accounting boundaries

LP Agent and Revert show that accounting definitions can be explicit. RANGE's existing audits show unresolved price, event and cost issues. The opportunity is not “invent PnL,” but display provenance, benchmark choice, missing flows and confidence.

**Gap strength:** medium. Valuable, but technically expensive if expanded to complete historical net returns.

### 4. Cross-tool workflow handoff

Users switch among discovery, analytics, alerts and execution. RANGE could provide a decision artifact and handoff to native Meteora/Jupiter tools without becoming a custodian.

**Gap strength:** medium. Distribution and partner willingness are unknown.

### 5. Consent-aware wallet intelligence

Smart-wallet discovery and copy products are active, but public pain evidence includes copying objections. A consent, attribution and uncertainty layer is more defensible than unrestricted copytrading.

**Gap strength:** low-medium. Demand and ethical/product constraints remain unresolved.

## Gaps not supported strongly enough

- A paid historical backtester with reliable net profitability.
- A universal best LP strategy.
- A market-wide LP leaderboard with comparable returns.
- An all-in-one platform as a better product than focused modules.
- Automated execution as a justified first build.
