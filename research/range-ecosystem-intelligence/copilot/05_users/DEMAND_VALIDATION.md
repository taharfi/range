# User Demand Validation

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-21  
**Primary dataset:** copied, unchanged `USER_PAIN_POINTS.csv` from the prior RANGE research

## Corpus

The existing source-qualified corpus contains **43 dated public records across 24 threads and 39 public handles**:

- 31 Meteora public governance records;
- 9 Meteora public GitHub issues;
- 3 Reddit public web records.

The records span 2024–2026, with 19 from 2024, 21 from 2025 and 3 from 2026. Multiple comments in one thread are separate authored reports but not independent buyer interviews. The dataset does not establish willingness to pay.

The requested 60 first-hand reports were **not reached** in this phase. X and private Discord evidence was not independently accessible enough for inclusion, Reddit's structured endpoint was blocked, and accessible strategy cards are not equivalent to pain reports. This is a coverage limit, not evidence that additional pain does not exist.

## Demand themes

| Theme | Existing count | Interpretation | Confidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Automation | 26 overlapping records | Manual exits, rebalancing and one-token flows recur; competitors now address much of the surface | Medium |
| Decision support | 18 overlapping records | Inventory, range, chart and metric interpretation are recurring needs | Medium |
| Smart/persistence | 12 overlapping records | Interest in reporting and configurable strategies; copy-harm objections are counterevidence | Low-medium |
| Backtesting | 6 overlapping records | Scenario/metric analysis appears, but no paid historical-backtester demand is established | Low |

## Validation interpretation

- Requests are evidence of friction, not proof of a market or subscription intent.
- Existing products materially reduce the original gap, so old requests must be revalidated against current workflows.
- The strongest near-term experiment is a read-only, cost-aware decision brief that measures whether users change a decision or return to it.
- Unrestricted copytrading is not validated: the corpus includes objections to unauthorized copying and harm from imitation.

## Next evidence needed

1. Current interviews or task-based tests with active LPs.
2. A willingness-to-pay test independent of compliments or feature requests.
3. Prospective decision outcomes, not retrospective self-reported returns.
4. Current users of LP Agent, MetEngine, HawkFi, Tidepool and native Meteora workflows.
