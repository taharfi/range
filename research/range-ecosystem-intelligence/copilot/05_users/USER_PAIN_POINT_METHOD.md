# User Pain-Point Method Note

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-21

The copied `USER_PAIN_POINTS.csv` is the unchanged prior RANGE corpus. It preserves source URLs, dates, thread identifiers, authors, evidence quality, hypothesis tags and current-resolution notes. The source report excludes promotions, bots, pure agreement, deleted posts and speculative grant proposals.

The corpus is purposive and forum-concentrated. It should be used to generate interview hypotheses, not to estimate the population of LPs or willingness to pay. “Current resolution” fields are not reinterpreted as proof that the problem is solved; they are retained for comparison against the current competitor and native-product evidence.
