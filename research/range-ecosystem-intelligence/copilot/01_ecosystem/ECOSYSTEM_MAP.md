# Solana LP Ecosystem Map: Initial Directory Pass

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-21  
**Primary discovery source:** [LP Army Community Tools](https://www.lparmy.com/community-tools)

## Directory observation

The LP Army community-tools page exposed 20 numbered listings in the browser snapshot, including MetEngine, Decoder Farmer, Rocket Scan, Fabriq, HawkFi, Metlex, Liquid Nova, UnifAI Network, Meteorology, CLOBr, Tokleo, DAMMIT, Metlab, DLMM Alert, Meridian, Ting and LP Agent. Some ordinal numbers were not visible in the captured text; the CSV records only names and links that were actually observed. The directory warns users to do their own research. It is a discovery index, not adoption proof.

## Ecosystem clusters

### Execution and automation

MetEngine, HawkFi, Liquid Nova, Meridian, Decoder Farmer and LP Agent advertise at least one of position opening, copying, automation, rebalancing or automated exits. This is the most crowded and security-sensitive cluster. The common claim is not equivalent to verified transaction reliability, custody safety or net returns.

### Analytics and discovery

Rocket Scan, Fabriq, CLOBr, Tokleo, Metlab and LP Agent advertise pool, wallet, price-level or performance intelligence. This overlaps directly with RANGE's current decision-support direction. A useful distinction must come from accounting quality, uncertainty communication, workflow outcomes or distribution rather than another generic dashboard.

### Alerts and education

DLMM Alert, Ting and Meteorology focus on monitoring, notifications or education. These are lighter-weight, read-only or low-custody surfaces and are potential integration/distribution partners rather than automatically direct competitors.

### Agent and data infrastructure

UnifAI Network, MetEngine Data Agent and Meridian point toward programmatic or AI-assisted strategy execution. Their public descriptions do not establish equivalent data quality, API stability or customer adoption.

## Strategic implication

The directory indicates that an “all-in-one LP platform” is not an empty category. Existing projects already cover discovery, smart-wallet signals, automation, alerts, copy trading and analytics in overlapping combinations. RANGE should avoid competing on feature count alone. The unresolved opportunity is whether a neutral, cost-aware, uncertainty-explicit decision layer can win trust before assuming custody or automated execution.
