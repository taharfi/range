## Implemented selection policy

`historical_prices.py` implements cached CoinGecko historical series and an optional Pyth authenticated historical-range adapter. Price source URL, response SHA256, sample timestamp, retrieval timestamp, mint, USD quote and quality status accompany every observation. Optional environment variables: `COINGECKO_DEMO_API_KEY` and `PYTH_API_KEY`; secrets are never logged or saved in request metadata.

The frozen verification policy accepts only positive, nonfuture prices no more than **60 seconds** old. Conflicting samples, future-only series, missing data and stale samples fail. There is no interpolation, stablecoin peg substitution or current-price fallback. A separate **3600-second sensitivity** is explicitly unverified and cannot populate verified P&L columns. Even a fresh aggregate price is a fair-value estimate, not actual historical swap execution proceeds.
